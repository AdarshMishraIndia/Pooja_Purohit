Account & Data Deletion

Understand how to delete your account and manage your personal data in the Pooja Purohit app.

🗑️
Self-Service

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Pooja Purohit account and associated profile data instantly, directly from within the app — no support contact required.

  1. 1
    Open the Pooja Purohit app and sign in to your account.
  2. 2
    Open the side navigation drawer from the top-left menu icon.
  3. 3
    Select Delete Account and confirm when prompted. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
⚠️ Account deletion is permanent. Your profile data — including name, phone number, email address, and device tokens — will be removed immediately and cannot be recovered.

Data Deletion Policy

The table below summarises what data is deleted, how it is handled, and the method required for each category.

Data Category What Gets Removed Method
Account Profile Name, phone number, email address, device / FCM tokens, unread notifications In-App (Instant)
Booking History Past ceremony bookings and priest assignments linked to your account Contact Support
Payment / Transaction Data Razorpay transaction records associated with your account Contact Support

Transaction and booking data may be retained for a period required by applicable financial regulations, even after a support-initiated deletion request. Retained data is used solely for compliance purposes and is not processed for any other use.
